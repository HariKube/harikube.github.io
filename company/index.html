The Product is the Vision, The Tool is HariKube

"It always seems impossible until it's done" - Nelson Mandela

We exist to unlock Kubernetes native application development. We remove the state bottlenecks that hold clusters back, including etcd bound limits, so teams can ship new services as CRDs and focus on product, not plumbing. We are not here to sell another infrastructure tool. We are here to champion a simpler model for cloud native architecture: declare intent through the Kubernetes API, let the platform handle state and scale, and keep your engineering effort where it belongs, on delivering value.
HariKube is our gift to the community: it is the engine that makes this practical. It strips away legacy state management constraints with a database agnostic data layer, so you can stop managing infrastructure and start orchestrating innovation.
